Global Refinery Capacity & Conversion Profile

Global refinery database assembled from the regional refinery CSV files. Unit capacities are shown in barrels per stream day (bpsd) where available in the source datasets.
Download CSV
Displayed process units: CDU, VDU, catalytic reformer, isomerization, alkylation, FCC, coker and hydrotreating. Previously blank process-unit capacities have been estimated. Estimated values are shown in italics with an asterisk (*). Estimates use the median unit/CDU capacity ratio for the same country when sufficient observations exist, otherwise the regional median, then the global median. Values are rounded to the nearest 1,000 bpsd.
