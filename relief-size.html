Calculation basis & source links

Equations, limits and state handling

Relieving P₁ = atmospheric pressure + set gauge pressure × (1 + selected overpressure fraction); backpressure P₂ is atmospheric pressure for ATM, or atmosphere + entered flare-header gauge pressure for FLARE. Total P₂ includes the entered header pressure only; outlet pipe losses must be included by the user. A = mass load / corrected mass flux. Gas: isentropic critical/subcritical nozzle relation with constant Cp/Cv and a user Z approximation. Liquid: mass flux = √(2ρΔP). Mixed-phase flux is supplied by the user. Two-phase HEM accepts a supplied isentropic equilibrium path; no internal EOS, flash, reactive/decomposition or flare-network solver is included. Thermal expansion uses βQ/Cp_liquid. Kv and total backpressure remain user-qualified inputs.

Pressure stress: cylindrical hoop stress = P D / (2 tₑ E); spherical membrane stress = P D / (4 tₑ E), with tₑ = nominal wall × (1 − mill tolerance) − corrosion allowance. This is an approximate thin-wall screen, only reported when D/tₑ ≥ 20. Allowable-stress interpolation uses the entered metal temperature; it is not a design-code thickness calculation. Metal mass = approximate geometric surface area × nominal thickness × metal density. Thermal capacity = mass × metal Cp. Blank stress values do not become zero.

All numerical state uses SI: K, Pa, m, kg/s, J/(kg·K), J/kg, Pa·s, m². Unit switching changes display only. Changing fluid reloads property seeds; changing metallurgy retains separate stress tables. Every input edit recalculates. Orifice areas are editable effective areas; catalog letter seeds require confirmation against the selected valve manufacturer.

Model assumptions and required data

Fire wetted area includes shell and heads below both the liquid level and the fire-height limit. The default 7.62 m (25 ft) limit and C_fire = 43,200 W/(m²)^0.82 require a justified fire scenario. F_env is entered; insulation, drainage and fireproofing credit are not automatically assigned.

The dry-gas fire case is a Cp × ΔT energy screen, not the API unwetted-vessel method. The gas transient assumes a rigid, well-mixed vapor-space volume, fixed Z/Cp/Cv, no condensation and zero feed. Liquid boiling / flashing replenishment is included in the separate steady fire PSV load Q/latent, not in the gas-only blowdown energy model. Outlet enthalpy is Cp × T. Integration uses bounded isentropic gas-expulsion substeps and implicit wall/fluid heat exchange. The open valve prevents expulsion from driving vessel pressure below the entered downstream pressure; heat exchange may subsequently change pressure. Wall exchange is coupled to fluid energy; wall heat storage uses constant metal Cp and nominal wall mass. Isothermal mode requires an implicit thermostat. Internal pressure is calculated from gas mass, temperature and volume, then used for stress. PSV reuse means an open blowdown area from t = 0, not a spring-loaded PSV opening simulation.

This revision sizes gas or flashed-vapor relief only. Bulk liquid and two-phase valve sizing are excluded, including HEM. Liquid and two-phase blowdown need a qualified external transient model and are explicitly blocked in the gas simulator. Mixture pseudo-critical values do not establish phase. All phase flags are user declarations.

Material names do not define grade, product form, heat treatment, code edition or temperature-dependent design allowable. Each material database retains editable T / allowable / yield / tensile rows; strengths start blank. Supply qualified values and source. No extrapolation is permitted; missing values prevent an integrity conclusion. Stress is a thin-shell membrane screen and excludes supports, nozzle loads, gradients, creep, buckling and fire damage.

CSV results contain displayed sizing results and every stored transient point. The HTML report includes current inputs, results, charts, material and mixture data and these assumptions. A selected letter is an API 526 effective-area seed; verify the manufacturer and certified coefficients. Backpressure is the entered total downstream pressure; no flare hydraulic network is solved.
