Calculation Basis

For fitted points, the workbook performs linear regression of ln(Cv) against ln(% open), giving Cv = a·(% open)n. Predicted fractional Cv is normalized to the specified full-open Cv. The surge calculation uses Kv = (29.9·Size² / Predicted Cv at 100%)² and FO Correction = (29.9·Size² / Open Cv)² − Kv. This HTML reproduces those formulas client-side.
