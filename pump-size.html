Centrifugal pump schematic — tank level control

Connected tank / pump / control valve • US units

Vertical vessel · V-101 / P-101 / LV-101

Tank bottom liquid outlet feeds pump suction; the level control valve is on the pump discharge. LT sends level to LIC, which signals the valve actuator. Rising tank level calls for increased outlet valve opening. The displayed level and valve opening are manual schematic settings; they do not change pump sizing inputs or simulate control dynamics. Hover equipment for sizing results in US units. Valve pressure drop and downstream pressure are not calculated.
