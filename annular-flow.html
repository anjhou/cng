Help / Model Basis

Required inputs: shaft diameter, radial clearance, bushing length, downstream pressure and either an upstream pressure or the workbook's calculated-pressure inputs. Liquid calculations additionally require density and viscosity. Gas calculations require molecular weight, specific-heat ratio, compressibility, temperature and gas viscosity.

Pressure modes: “Input” uses the entered upstream pressure. “Calculate” follows the worksheet relation: bushing pressure = suction pressure + relative bushing pressure × total dynamic head expressed as pressure.

Implementation note: the Excel flow cell calls a compiled VBA UDF named bushingflow; its source code is not exposed as worksheet formulas. The HTML therefore uses an engineering annular-flow implementation calibrated to the workbook's saved liquid benchmark (24,148.66 lb/hr at 60 psia bushing pressure for the default Unit Set 2 case). Treat gas results and off-design extrapolation as screening estimates unless independently checked against the original Excel/VBA model.
