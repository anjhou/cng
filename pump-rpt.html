Documentation

Workbook scope

This tool is derived from the workbook sheets Input_output, SI Calculation, Tables, Constants, User Defined Units and Revisions. The workbook performs the governing calculations in SI and converts display values to the selected unit set.

Reciprocating-pump basis

The displaced volume per revolution is Vrev = π/4 · Dplunger² · Stroke · Number of heads · Acting factor. Theoretical flow is Vrev · rotational speed, and volumetric efficiency is rated flow divided by theoretical flow.

Acceleration head

The workbook uses hacc = L · v · n(rpm) · C / (K · g), where C is selected from the pump-head/acting table and K is the liquid relative-compressibility factor. Acceleration pressure is pacc = hacc · g · ρ. Minimum and maximum instantaneous pressures are the mean pressure minus/plus acceleration pressure.

NPSHA

Steady-state NPSHA is based on suction pressure minus vapor pressure plus velocity head. The undamped NPSHA uses the minimum suction pressure after acceleration-pressure deduction.

Pulsation dampener

For admitted residual pulsation y, the workbook sets P1=(1−y)Pt and P2=(1+y)Pt. Dampener volume is V0 = Vrev·C/1.25 · (1/precharge)^(1/γ) / [1−(P1/P2)^(1/γ)]. The workbook uses γ=1.4 for air or nitrogen and notes an 80% pre-charge ratio as standard.

Assumptions and limitations

The suction acceleration-head method is noted in the workbook as valid up to approximately 15 m / 50 ft line length; use virtual length where more than one diameter is present. The workbook does not provide viscosity, temperature, elevation, friction-factor, slip, or user-entered dampener-volume terms in its governing formulas, so this conversion does not invent them.

Chart methodology

The workbook chart uses a cycle length of 360/(number of heads × acting factor) degrees and five pressure points: mean, minimum, mean, maximum, mean. Both suction and discharge are shown with and without dampening.
