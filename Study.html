Five characteristics of a high-quality USGC study

PrincipleStudy focus
Business drivenStart with what creates value, not what can be built.
Realistic brownfield assessmentPiping congestion, tie-ins, structural loading, utilities and turnaround requirements.
Strong operations involvementValidate operational practicality with operations and maintenance personnel.
Early construction inputAccess, labor, modularization and turnaround windows; construction may exceed 40–50% of TIC on some projects.
Credible cost and scheduleDefined scope and assumptions, benchmarking and identified risks.

Investment decision

Proceed

Preferred option has credible economics and manageable risks; advance to FEED.

Recycle

Resolve material uncertainties or rework alternatives before gate approval.

Stop

Business case or feasibility does not justify further investment.
