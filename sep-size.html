Method & limitations

Inputs and output categories are modeled on the supplied SepSiz workbooks. This independent browser implementation uses a transparent Souders–Brown vapor-capacity estimate, liquid holdup and circular-segment geometry. The original workbook's VBA-defined proprietary routines, mesh efficiency, two-phase regime maps, nozzle rules and pressure-drop correlations have not been replicated or verified. Do not assume numerical equivalence with SepSiz. Obtain mechanical and internals-vendor confirmation before equipment specification.
