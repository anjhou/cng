Engineering basis and validation status

Important: This is an independent engineering implementation based on the workbook's visible inputs and formulas. The workbook relies on VBA/UDF functions (including COMGAS_Case1 and COMGAS_Case7) for its compressible pipe and orifice calculations; those routines have not been independently reproduced. Accordingly, numerical equivalence with Excel has not been demonstrated. The displayed pressure ratio is an isentropic static-to-stagnation relation, not a general upstream-to-downstream pressure relation. Verify discharge coefficients, friction correlations, compressibility, Joule–Thomson behavior and low-temperature material limits before design or relief studies.
