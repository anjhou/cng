Methodology

Initial TIC is sampled from a normal distribution conditional on positive cost. Annual net cash flow (revenue less OPEX) is normally distributed; negative values are permitted. TIC and cash flow draws are independent. Persistent mode uses one cash flow draw per project; independent mode draws separately each year. NPV is cumulative discounted net cash flow. VIR is discounted positive cash flows divided by discounted negative cash flows. IRR is reported only for conventional cash flows (no negative operating cash flow); payback is the first cumulative cash flow crossing of zero, interpolated within the year. Unrecovered paybacks are excluded from payback statistics and reported separately. ROI is cumulative net cash flow divided by initial TIC. No taxes, terminal value or financing are modeled.
