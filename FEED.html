Cost estimate and execution basis

Indicative estimate accuracy and FEED investment

Class 4: ±15% to ±30%; Class 3: ±10% to ±20%. These are illustrative ranges supplied for planning, not guaranteed accuracy or formal AACE classification limits. Actual uncertainty depends on scope definition, technology, market conditions, risk and owner standards. FEED may represent approximately 1–3% of total installed cost for large projects; actual expenditure varies.

Ethylene crackers · FCC units · Hydrocrackers · Delayed cokers · LNG facilities · PDH units · Amine systems · Utilities and offsites
