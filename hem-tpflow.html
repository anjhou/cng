Calculation assumptions and validation

The Excel workbook includes custom VBA functions and cached results, which cannot be reliably reconstructed from worksheet formulas alone. This HTML preserves the visible property-state inputs, performs pressure-dependent property interpolation and independent HEM/separated-flow estimates, and shows interactive property plots. Its model outputs and chart appearance are not claimed to be numerically or visually identical to the workbook. Validate against the workbook and an approved two-phase hydraulic simulator before equipment sizing, relief design, or safety-critical use.
