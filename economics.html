Calculation basis

NPV = sum of annual net cash flows discounted to Year 0. IRR is the rate at which NPV equals zero; multiple-sign-change cash flows may have multiple IRRs and are flagged. MIRR uses discounted negative cash flows and reinvested positive cash flows over 20 years. Simple and discounted payback are the first recovery of cumulative cash flow to zero after an initial deficit, interpolated within the year. Cumulative ROI = cumulative net cash flow / cumulative CAPEX × 100. No tax, financing, depreciation or terminal value is modeled separately.
